Selected publications

IEEE TMC2024

Energy Optimization for Mobile Applications by Exploiting 5G Inactive State

Zhi Ding, Yuxiang Lin, Weifeng Xu, Jiamei Lv, Yi Gao, Wei Dong

IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing · 2024

A phone’s 5G radio can keep drawing power after an app finishes a transfer. 5GSaver predicts when the next packet will arrive so the phone can choose a lower-power state between transfers. Across 15 apps on commercial smartphones, it reduced radio energy use by 9.5% and communication delay by 12.4% on average compared with the reference method.

Providing UE-level QoS Support by Joint Scheduling and Orchestration for 5G vRAN

Jiamei Lv, Yi Gao, Zhi Ding, Yuxiang Lin, Xinyun You, Guang Yang, Wei Dong

IEEE Conference on Computer Communications · Vancouver, 2024

Implemented on OpenAirInterface, UQ-vRAN coordinates radio resources and network functions to meet each device’s service requirements. Across the tested network settings, it reduced the rate of missed delay targets by 12–41% compared with prior approaches.

CWSN2024

SmartTask: Efficient Dispatching for Low-Latency Tasks on Dynamic Edge Networks

Zhi Ding, Boyu Kong, Jiamei Lv, Yi Gao, Wei Dong

CWSN 2024 · Springer proceedings, 2025 · pp. 64–75

SmartTask probes the network to estimate transmission delay and uses a neural network to predict processing time. A reinforcement learning policy uses both estimates to choose an edge server for each task. In a simulation with 50 clients and 200 tasks, average end-to-end latency fell by 26–86%, depending on the baseline.
